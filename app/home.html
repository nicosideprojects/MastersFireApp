Disaster and Resource Monitoring System

Browser-based geospatial decision support for fire response and infrastructure planning. Choose a mode to begin.

Operations Mode
Real-time incident response. Place fire, truck, person, and aerial pins to get live resource recommendations, road-network routing, and a wind-aware fire-spread cone.
Analysis Mode
Coverage-gap planning. Visualise hydrant service-area buffers, identify infrastructure gaps, and get candidate sites for new hydrants via MCLP analysis.
